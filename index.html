🎖️ WARBOX 3D — How to play

  • Goal: sandbox war simulator. Spawn Blue vs Red armies, they fight automatically. Last team standing wins.
  • Spawn: pick a tool on the left, then left-click the ground. Click fast — dragging orbits the camera instead.
  • Camera: drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, right-drag to pan. WASD/QE flies the camera.
  • Possess 🎮: select it, click one of your Blue units, then drive it with WASD + mouse, click / Space to shoot, Esc to release.
  • Quick battle: spawns two 8-soldier + 2-tank armies. Airstrike: click anywhere to call an explosion.
  • Mines 💣 explode when enemies walk near. Crates/walls block movement and bullets.
  • Speed toggles 1×/2×/3× simulation. P pauses.
