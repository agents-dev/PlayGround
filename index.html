SQUARED CIRCLE SHOWDOWN

Arcade wrestling: punch, kick, slam, pin. First pinfall or KO wins.

Player 1: AD move · W jump · S block · J punch · K kick · L slam · F finisher · P pin/taunt · T taunt

2P local: ◀▶ move · ▲ jump · ▼ block · 1 punch · 2 kick · 3 slam · 4 fin · 5 pin

Knock your rival down with slams & finishers. Stand close over a downed rival and press PIN. Attacker mashes PIN, defender mashes JUMP to escape. Survive the 3-count! Health = 0 is a KO. Full yellow bar = FINISHER.

🎙️ Commentary: Welcome to the sold-out arena! Bell rings on match start.
💡 Tips: Blocking drains stamina but negates 70%. Slams need to be close. Taunt (T) restores stamina + momentum but leaves you open. Jump + Kick = diving kick. Work the body, then PIN when they're down!
🏆 Win by: Pinfall (3-count), Knockout (HP → 0), or Decision (more HP when clock hits 0). R restart · M mute. Click canvas to enable sound.
