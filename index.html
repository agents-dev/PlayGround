How to win

  1. Build unit spawners on your side of the map.
  2. Spawners auto-send units down lanes toward the enemy Nexus.
  3. Kills pay bounty minerals. Spend them on more spawners.
  4. Destroy the enemy Nexus. Lose yours and the game ends.

Economy: kills + income

Every ~20s you get income. Unit buildings give units and income. Pylons give only income — but more of it.

Pro tips: wall · mid · sync

🧱 Wall one lane

Stack buildings vertically to seal a lane so your whole ground army funnels into the other lane. Never leak ground from the walled lane.

↔ Mid reinforcements

Build on the center line and rally to either lane. Best for air (Carriers, Mutas, Brood Lords) and heavy ground (Tanks, Ultras, Archons).

🌊 Sync waves

Cancel production with ESC so units spawn together on the 10s timer. One big wave beats a trickle — except casters (Infestor, Viper, Sentry).

☢ Save your nuke

One nuke per game, defensive range only. Use it to stop a Nexus-threatening push, not to pad kills.

Pick a race fantasy

Protoss: Immortals early, Archons mid, Carrier + Mothership Core deathball late. Upgrade shields first.
