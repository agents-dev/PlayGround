How to play

• Each turn you get 30 seconds: run into position, pick a weapon, aim and fire. Firing ends your move phase.
• Hold Space or FIRE to charge power, release to shoot. Angle ↑↓ (10°–85°), direction ←→.
• Wind pushes Bazooka / Grenade shells. Positive = blows toward +X.
• Weapons: 💥 Bazooka (guided by wind, big boom) · 🟢 Grenade (bounces, 3s fuse) · 🔫 Shotgun (2 close-range hits) · ✈️ Airstrike (5 bombs in a line) · 🌀 Teleport (free reposition).
• Worms take fall damage over hard drops and drown instantly in water.
• Crates heal +25 HP. Gold crates give +1 teleport.
