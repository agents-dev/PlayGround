STARCRAFT 3D

NEON FRONTLINE

Command the Terran Expeditionary Force against the Swarm. Harvest crystals & gas, raise depots & war factories, mass Marines, Tanks, Wraiths & Battlecruisers — and raze the Hive before the Swarm overruns you.

VICTORY

FIELD MANUAL

Goal
Destroy every Swarm structure (Hatcheries, Spires, Sunkens). Lose if you have no buildings and no workers.
Economy
SCVs harvest minerals (+5/trip) and gas (+4/trip, needs Refinery). Right-click a crystal/geyser, then right-click your Command Center area to auto-return (auto by default).
Buildings (select SCV → B)
⛽ Refinery (75⚙) · 🏠 Depot +8 supply (100⚙) · 🪖 Barracks (150⚙) · 🏭 Factory (200⚙+100⛽) · ✈ Starport (250⚙+150⛽) · 🔫 Turret (100⚙)
Army
Barracks: Marine (50⚙) · Factory: Siege Tank (150⚙+50⛽) · Starport: Wraith (150⚙+75⛽), Battlecruiser (400⚙+250⛽). Air units fly over ground & can't be hit by melee.
Tips
Keep SCVs pumping early. 1 Barracks → Factory → Starport. Turrets guard ramps. Attack-move (A) into the red zone. Control groups win wars.
