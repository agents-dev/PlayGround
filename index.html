⚔️ WARCRAFT 3D

A tiny browser RTS — humans vs orcs • Three.js • no assets, all procedural

🛖 Build: Town Hall trains Peasants. Barracks trains Footmen & Archers. Farms give supply. Towers defend.
⛏️ Gather: right-click gold mine / trees with a peasant. They return to the Hall automatically.
⚔️ Fight: drag-select your army, right-click orcs to attack, or press A then click for attack-move.
🏆 Win: destroy the Orc Stronghold in the north-east. Lose: if your Town Hall falls. Survive the waves!
