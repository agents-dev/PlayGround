How to play WARBOX

Paint armies onto the field, then watch them fight. You are the god of this battlefield: spawn, bomb, erase, repeat.

  • Pick a team (Red / Blue), pick a unit, then click or drag on the field to spawn.
  • Bomb (click = explosion), Airstrike (click = carpet-bomb line), Wall (drag = sandbags), Eraser (drag = delete).
  • Camera: mouse wheel = zoom, right-drag or WASD / arrows = pan, 0 = reset view.
  • Keys: 1-4 units · T switch team · B bomb · V airstrike · C wall · X eraser · Space pause · L slow-mo · F 2x · M mute.
  • Units fight on their own: soldiers, heavies, snipers and tanks all have different range / damage / health.
